Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection concerning personal data processed by MatchOracle.

Submit a request

  1. Open MatchOracle on your iPhone.
  2. Open the app menu and select In-App Purchase Help.
  3. Tap Contact Support. The draft email includes the anonymous Support ID needed to locate backend records.
  4. State the request you want to make and the country or region where you live.

You may also email matchoracleapp@gmail.com. If the Support ID is not included, we may be unable to identify anonymous installation records.

Do not send payment-card details, passwords, Apple Account credentials, government identification, or unrelated sensitive information. We may request limited additional verification when reasonably necessary to protect the requester and other users.

What deletion does not do

Deleting MatchOracle data or uninstalling the app does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel Premium through Apple subscription settings.

Apple controls App Store purchase, billing, and refund records. To request an eligible refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Limits and timing

We respond within the period required by applicable law. A request may be limited where retention is required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, unresolved transactions or disputes, or protection of another person’s rights. We will explain an applicable limitation unless prohibited by law.

Other choices

  • Disable local notifications in MatchOracle or iOS Settings.
  • Remove local preferences and caches by deleting the app.
  • Manage or cancel Premium in Apple subscription settings.
  • Contact the competent data-protection authority available under the law that applies to you.
